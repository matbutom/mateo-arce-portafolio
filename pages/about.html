mateo arce is a designer and programmer based in Santiago, Chile, currently taking the Magíster en Artes Mediales at Universidad de Chile. He studied design at Universidad Diego Portales, and co-founded Rafita Studio, a small practice mixing branding, illustration, and a probably unreasonable number of dog drawings.

His work moves between graphic design, creative coding, and small hardware experiments, usually by building the tools he needs along the way — from browser-based generators and interfaces to a diy pixel camera. He's designed and developed websites, mapped projection animations for Teatro Municipal de Santiago, and exhibited interactive work at Museo Interactivo Mirador.

He's as interested in teaching and sharing process as he is in making — breaking design and code down into something playable, mostly through p5.js and Processing. Always up for talking dogs, typefaces, or weird little tools. Say hello~
